01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Ideas into
impact.

I help organizations understand places, listen to people and turn good ideas into plans that work.

Urban planning practitioner with a Master of Urban Planning from NYU Wagner and a background in transportation planning, stakeholder engagement and GIS and spatial analysis. I have worked across municipal planning, state housing research and national advocacy, turning research and community input into clear, practical recommendations.

I bring tools like ArcGIS, QGIS, R and STATA together with years of experience facilitating workshops and public engagement sessions. I am especially interested in mapping where resources and needs do not line up, then working out how to close those gaps.
